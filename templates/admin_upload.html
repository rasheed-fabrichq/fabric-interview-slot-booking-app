Upload once for the whole drive. Candidates are not tied to a case study — each candidate picks which one they want when they open their booking link, and may book only one interview in total.
File Requirements:
  • Format: CSV or Excel (.xlsx, .xls)
  • Required columns: Name, Email, AI Interview Link (column names are not case-sensitive)
  • The candidate ID is read from the candidate_id parameter of the AI Interview Link and must be a valid UUID
  • Any job in the link is ignored — candidates choose their case study when booking
Sample CSV Format
Name,Email,AI Interview Link
