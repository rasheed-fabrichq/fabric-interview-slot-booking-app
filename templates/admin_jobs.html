Batches: different slots for different candidates of one job
  • Use Add batch when some of a job's candidates should see different dates or times.
  • Each batch has its own dates, slots, capacity and emails. The original job counts as the first batch.
  • Upload each batch's candidates into that batch. Their links and booking links stay the same; a candidate sees only their own batch's slots.
